?# User Guide — EduSolve AI

Two guides: for **students** using the site, and for the **admin operator** running the console.

---

# Part A — Student Guide

## A1. What is EduSolve AI?

EduSolve AI gives you a clear, step-by-step answer when an online college portal lets you down — a scholarship form that will not submit, an OTP that never arrives, a photo that keeps getting rejected, a payment that left your account but produced no receipt, or a page refresh that wiped everything you typed.

Everything runs in your own browser. You do not need an account, and no file you choose is ever uploaded.

---

## A2. Getting around

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The starting point. Big "Start Solving" button, 4 quick actions, popular categories, and a 3-step explanation of how the site works. |
| **Dashboard** | The full problem library. Pick a problem type from 12 tabs, read the solution, and track your progress on a checklist. |
| **AI Problem Solver** | The chat. Describe your problem in your own words and get guided. |
| **Smart Tools** | Six tabs of practical helpers: check a file's size and format, resize a photo or signature, and work through checklists. |
| **Project Report** | The complete project report — abstract, objectives, architecture, algorithm, testing, viva questions — with a print button and a Markdown download. |

On a phone, the four screens are the buttons at the **bottom** of the screen (the raised round button in the middle opens the Solver). On a computer, the same links are in the **left sidebar**. The top bar has the language switch (English ⇄ मराठी) on every screen.

---

## A3. Switching the language

Tap the **मराठी / English** button in the top bar or the sidebar. The whole interface changes immediately, and your choice is remembered the next time you open the site. All 12 problem categories, every solution, every checklist and every follow-up question exist in both languages.

---

## A4. Using the AI Problem Solver

1. Open **AI Problem Solver**.
2. Type your problem in plain words. Example: *"My scholarship form is not submitting"*.
   - You can write in English or Marathi.
   - You do not need technical terms.
   - If the site does not understand, it will simply ask you to rephrase.
3. The assistant identifies the problem type and asks **two quick questions**. Tap the option that fits your situation.
4. Your answer appears in four parts:
   - **Possible causes** — what is probably happening
   - **Step-by-step solution** — the fix, in order
   - **Prevention tips** — how to avoid it next time
   - **Alternative solution** — what to do if the fix does not work
5. Tap **Problem Solved** or **Not Solved**.
   - *Solved* records a helpful result.
   - *Not Solved* gives you the next best step — usually opening the closest category in the Dashboard or contacting the college helpdesk with screenshots.

There are also 5 one-tap **example prompts** if you would rather not type, and a **Clear** button to start a fresh conversation.

---

## A5. Using the Dashboard

- **Pick a problem type.** A tab strip at the top of the page lists all **12 problem types**. Tap the tab that matches your problem and its full solution opens immediately. On a phone the strip scrolls sideways, so swipe it to see every tab.
- **The 12 category cards** below the strip do the same thing — tap a card and the full solution opens.
- **Recently viewed** — the last 6 categories you opened.
- **Saved solutions** — tap **Save** on any solution to keep it here.
- **Task progress & checklists** — 5 pre-form tasks (Aadhaar and marksheet ready, documents under the size limit, photo and signature ready, draft saved, preview checked) with a progress bar.

> If your problem does not fit any tab, use the **Ask AI** button or the **AI Problem Solver** and describe it in your own words — the solver accepts free text and picks the closest category for you.

## A6. Smart Tools explained

The six tools sit in a **tab strip** at the top of the page. Tap a tab to open that tool — all six are one tap away. Inside a tool, small choices (the file preset, the photo quality) are made with a short row of **pill buttons**.

### 1. Document preparation checklist
Five things to have ready before you start any form. Tick them off; your progress is saved.

### 2. File extension and size checker — *this one really works*
1. Tap the preset you need in the **Preset** switcher:
   - PDF for scholarship documents — max **200 KB**
   - Passport photo JPG — max **50 KB**
   - Signature JPG — max **30 KB**
   - General image — max **200 KB**
2. Choose a file from your device.
3. Get an instant verdict on the format and the size.

Your file is **read on your device only**. It is never uploaded anywhere, and nothing leaves your phone or laptop.

*If it fails:* re-scan the document at **150 DPI in black and white**, or use the Photo Resizer below to shrink it.

### 3. Photo and signature guidance + resizer — *this one really works*
1. Take the photo properly: **white background, daylight, face straight**. For a signature: **black pen on plain paper**.
2. Choose the photo.
3. Set the width and height in pixels (200 × 230 is the usual passport-photo size).
4. Tap the quality in the **Quality** switcher: **0.6** (smallest), **0.8** (balanced) or **0.92** (clearest).
5. Press **Resize**. The app shows the exact output size in KB.
6. Press **Download resized JPG** and upload that file to your portal.

If the output is larger than your original photo, the app warns you that it will look soft — in that case pick a size close to your original photo.

### 4. Form-filling checklist
Read the instruction PDF fully first, copy your name and date of birth letter-by-letter from your Aadhaar, fill every field marked `*`, test your mobile number and email, and press **Save Draft** after every page.

### 5. Pre-submission verification
Scroll the preview page fully and check for red errors, open the photo and signature previews, tick the declaration checkbox, save the payment receipt or UTR, and be ready to download the acknowledgement.

### 6. Required-document checklist
Pick **Scholarship**, **Admission** or **Exam** and tick off every document that list asks for.

## A7. Installing the app on your phone

**Android (Chrome):** open the site → tap the ⋮ menu → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
**iPhone (Safari):** tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
**Windows/Edge:** click the install icon in the address bar.

Installed, it opens like a normal app and keeps working even with a weak connection.

## A8. Safety rules — please read these

1. **Never give your password, OTP, CVV or bank PIN to anyone** — not to EduSolve, not to a "helpline", not to an agent. EduSolve will never ask for them.
2. **Never pay an agent who promises "guaranteed approval".** Use only the official links on the college or government portal.
3. **If money was deducted but you got no receipt:** check your bank or UPI app for the transaction, save the **UTR / transaction reference number**, and contact the official helpline listed on the portal with it. EduSolve cannot confirm payments.
4. **If a form will not submit:** take a screenshot of the error message before you close the page. Screenshots are what the college office actually needs.

## A9. Reading the Project Report

The **Project Report** tab (in the navbar, the sidebar and the bottom tab bar) holds the graphical analytics report.

- **Slim toolbar at the top** — Print / PDF, the two downloads and the two filter switches. There is no text header; the numbers start immediately below.
- **KPI cards** — problems reported, problems solved, problems pending, solve rate, solved per day and the most frequent problem, each with a sparkline where a trend exists.
- **Charts** — solved vs pending (donut), solve rate (gauge), problems solved in one day (stacked columns), most frequently generated problems (ranked bars), solve rate per problem type, cumulative problems solved (area), weekly comparison and where the problems are reported from.
- **Tables** — the pending "help desk" list and a day-wise result table with solved, pending, total and rate for every day.
- **Data source and time window** — switch between *All data*, *Project dataset* and *This device*, and between the last 7, 14 or 30 days. Everything re-computes instantly.
- **Print / Save as PDF** — the print button opens your browser's print dialog with a clean A4 layout: the navigation is hidden and the charts are laid out two per row. Choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.
- **Download Excel (.xlsx)** — saves the current report as a real Excel workbook with five sheets: **Summary**, **Daily**, **By Problem Type**, **By Source** and **Weekly**. The numbers stay numeric (so you can sum them in Excel), the header row is frozen and every label follows the language you are viewing. **Full document (.md)** downloads the full written project document.
- Everything switches between English and Marathi with the global toggle, and the numbers are computed live in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## A10. Frequently asked questions

**Does EduSolve connect to my college portal?**
No. It is a guidance app. It does not log in to, read from, or repair any third-party website.

**Is this real AI?**
It is a rule-based system that matches your words against a keyword list for each problem type and then walks you through a proven checklist. It is honest about that, and it works offline.

**Will my files be uploaded?**
Never. The file checker reads the file name and size from your device. The resizer draws on an on-page canvas. Nothing is transmitted.

**My problem is not in the 12 categories. What now?**
Use the Solver and describe it anyway — the "Other" category gives a general 5-step method (write it in one line, screenshot the error, check the Notice/Circular section, visit the college office or a CSC centre with your Aadhaar and application ID, and never share passwords or OTPs).

**Will my progress be saved?**
Yes, on the device you are using. Your language choice, saved solutions, recently viewed items, checklist ticks and the solved/not-solved records all stay in your browser. Clearing your browser data erases them.

**Can I use it in Marathi?**
Yes — the entire site, including all 12 categories and every step, is available in मराठी.

# Part B — Admin / Operator Guide

## B1. What the admin console is

A **separate** web app, deliberately isolated from the student site, for the person who looks after the project. It shows how students are using the guidance, which problems are solved, and lets you manage content flags.

It has **no server, no database and no real accounts**. Everything it shows comes from the browser it is opened in.

---

## B2. Signing in

1. Open the admin URL.
2. Enter any name (it is only used as a label).
3. Enter the demo PIN: **`1234`**
4. Press **Sign in**.

The session is stored in `localStorage` under `edusolve_admin`. **Log out** from the sidebar, the top bar or Settings.

> For a real deployment, put the admin site behind a password (Vercel Password Protection, Netlify Basic Auth, Cloudflare Access) and change the hard-coded PIN check in `admin-app/src/App.jsx`.

---

## B3. The four tabs

### Overview
- **4 KPI cards** — feedback entries, solved rate, number of problem categories, and visits over the last 7 days.
- **Daily visits** — a 7-day bar chart. Bar height is proportional to the largest day.
- **Recent feedback** — the last 5 records with their verdict, source and a relative timestamp.
- **Breakdown** — a donut gauge showing solved vs. open, plus a horizontal split bar and a count by source (Dashboard / Solver).

### Feedback
- The complete inbox of Solved / Not Solved records.
- **Search** by problem title.
- **Filter** by All / Solved / Open.
- **Clear** removes every record (there is a confirmation step).

### Categories
- Lists all 12 problem categories with their icons.
- **Search** by name.
- **Toggle Featured** on any category with the switch. Featured categories are flagged in the database for a future "popular" section.
- Flags live in this browser only — the note under the table says so explicitly.

### Settings
- **Session** — who is signed in, plus Log out.
- **Local data** — **Reset data** wipes the feedback, visits and flags (but keeps the language choice).
- **Build info** — a reminder that this is a separate React + Vite app with no backend.
- **Safety** — never enter real passwords, OTPs, UPI or bank details.

---

## B4. The admin language toggle

The admin console has its **own** language switch, independent of the student site. An operator can run the cabinet in Marathi while the student site stays in English.

---

## B5. Understanding the numbers

| Metric | Meaning | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Feedback entries** | Solved / Not Solved clicks | Only from this browser |
| **Solved rate** | `solved ÷ total × 100` | Rounds to the nearest whole percent |
| **Visits, 7 days** | Page loads per day | Counted once per page load, per day key |
| **Categories** | `CATEGORIES.length` = 12 | Live from the shared data module |

On the very first login the console seeds **5 realistic demo feedback entries** (spanning 2 to 44 hours) so the dashboard is never empty during a presentation. Real feedback is appended to the same list, and the seed never runs again.

---

## B6. Operating notes

- **Feedback is per-origin.** If the admin console is served from a different host or port than the student site, it will not see the student site's feedback — it will show only its own seeded data. Serve them from the same origin if you want the live link.
- **Backing up:** the data is in the browser's `localStorage`. To export it, open DevTools → *Application* → *Local Storage* → copy the JSON for `edusolve_feedback`.
- **Resetting:** Settings → Reset data is the cleanest way to start a fresh demo. It preserves the language preference.
- **Never commit** `dist`, `dist-admin` or `node_modules`.

---

## B7. Admin FAQ

**Can I add or edit categories from the admin?**
Not yet — content is edited in `src/data/categories.js` and redeployed. The Categories tab currently manages the *Featured* flag only, and says so in its footnote.

**How do I change the demo PIN?**
`admin-app/src/App.jsx`, the line `if (pin.trim() !== '1234')`. Change it there and rebuild.

**Why is the admin a separate app?**
So the student bundle stays small and the admin interface never appears in student navigation. It was split out in commit `03b17af`.

**Does the console work offline?**
The admin app has no service worker, but it is a static SPA — once loaded it works offline, though it reads and writes only local browser data.
